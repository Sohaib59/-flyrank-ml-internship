## 1. Where the next case study goes, and how to add one

**Location:** [e.g. `/case-studies/` folder in the portfolio repo, or a "Projects" section on
your site at yoursite.com/work — name the exact place]

**The three-beat shape (reused from Week 2):**
1. **Problem** — what was broken or missing, in one or two sentences.
2. **What I did** — the real decision(s), not a feature list.
3. **What came of it** — the result, a number if you have one, or an honest "still in progress."

**Steps to add a new case, every time:**
1. Open [the Claude Project — see §3] and paste in whatever you have: a README, a demo link, a
   few rough notes about what you built.
2. Ask it to draft the three-beat write-up in your existing voice — it already has your prior
   case studies as reference, so this is editing, not starting from a blank page.
3. Drop the draft into [the exact file/CMS field — e.g. `case-studies/new-project.md` or your
   site builder's "add project" form].
4. [Any build/deploy step your site needs — e.g. `git push`, or click Publish.]
5. Sanity-check the live page once it's up.

That's the whole loop — five steps, none of them "rebuild the site."
